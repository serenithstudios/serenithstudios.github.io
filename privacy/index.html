Primal Wars privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026. Serenith Studios is the brand of Serenith LLC. This notice describes the current Primal Wars app and the information used to provide saved games, purchases and support.

Saved games and iCloud

Primal Wars requires internet access and an available iCloud account. The app saves your progress, tutorial state, inventory, currencies, upgrades, challenge activity, reward claims and purchase records on your device and in your private CloudKit database. The complete save synchronizes to your devices using the same iCloud account. Sound, haptic and display preferences remain on each device.

The save includes a pseudonymous iCloud account binding, transaction identifiers, save dates and device model (such as iPhone or iPad). These are used to associate the correct save, recover purchases and prevent duplicate rewards or accidental replacement. The app does not read your Apple Account password or iCloud email address. We do not operate a separate server that receives your private game save, and private CloudKit records are not available to us through the developer dashboard.

Purchases and Game Center

Apple processes purchases. The app receives verified product and transaction information, purchase dates, refund status and an account token derived from the iCloud account binding. It uses that information to deliver purchases and recover the correct monthly Trail Pass, 30-Day VIP and Permanent Ad Skip entitlements. We do not receive your card number or other payment credentials.

Game Center is optional. If you connect, the app displays your Game Center player name and provides access to Apple's Game Center interface. Game Center sign-in is separate from iCloud save ownership. Apple operates these services under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

Starting with build 8, Primal Wars includes optional rewarded ads provided by Google Mobile Ads. Google may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash reports and performance information to provide ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. We configure non-personalized ads, disable the publisher first-party advertising ID and disable SDK collection through device location services. The app does not request advertising tracking permission or access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Development and TestFlight use Google test ads. Live ads will be enabled only after publisher setup. Where required, a privacy message appears before requesting ads, and an “Ad privacy choices” entry in Settings lets you revisit available choices. Ads remain optional; declining or closing an incomplete ad does not consume a reward allowance. Permanent Ad Skip grants eligible rewards without requesting an ad. See Google’s privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

We do not include a separate third-party analytics SDK or sell your game save to data brokers. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy notices.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information, and remove personal details from screenshots. Support email is handled using our email service provider. We retain correspondence while handling your request and as needed for legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations.

Retention and your choices

Your local save remains until the app's data is removed. The app keeps up to eight local recovery snapshots before older snapshots are removed. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete its iCloud save. You can manage or remove iCloud app data through your device's Apple Account storage controls; removing it can permanently remove progress. Apple purchase records are managed separately by Apple. To keep playing and syncing, iCloud must remain available.

You can change device preferences in the app and manage Game Center and Apple privacy choices in your device settings. For questions or requests about information you have sent directly to the studio, contact contact@serenithstudios.com. We cannot recover a private iCloud save that has been permanently removed.

Changes

We will update this notice when the app's data practices change. New features that introduce data collection will be described here and reflected in the app's App Store privacy information.
